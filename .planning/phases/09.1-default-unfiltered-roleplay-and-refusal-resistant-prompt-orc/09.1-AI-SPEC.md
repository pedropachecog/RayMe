## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Conversational

**Description:**
RayMe composes and streams character-roleplay requests to model-agnostic
OpenAI-compatible endpoints, primarily an offline Qwen model served by
llama-server. Good behavior means an inspectable effective request, faithful
in-character continuation by default, bounded recovery from generic policy
refusals, and accepted text reaching live-call speech before generation ends.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. A generic AI/guideline refusal reaches captions, speech, or durable history.
2. Refusal detection suppresses ordinary in-character negation or dialogue.
3. Prefix recovery waits for a whole response and destroys live-call latency.
4. Text, message-action, preview, and call paths construct different requests.
5. Interrupt or retry races permit rejected or late text/audio to escape.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Private, offline character roleplay and interactive
fiction with phone-like live conversation

**User Population:** A single roleplay enthusiast on a trusted LAN, including
users accustomed to SillyTavern-class character cards, who continues fictional
scenes through text, message actions, and live calls.

**Stakes Level:** Medium

**Output Consequence:** The generated turn is rendered immediately, may be
spoken as the character while generation is still active, and is then persisted
as canonical context for later text and call turns. A bad turn therefore breaks
immersion now and can distort the continuing scene; this is a creative-product
consequence rather than a health, legal, financial, or other regulated decision.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

**Dimension: Character embodiment and in-world voice**

**Good (domain expert would accept):** The reply is recognizably this character:
its facts, relationships, motives, diction, emotional posture, actions, and
inner thoughts fit the card and current scene. It gives only the next in-world
turn, with no AI identity, prompt explanation, policy reference, or editorial
preamble.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The reply could come from any helpful
assistant, contradicts a durable trait or relationship, moralizes or sanitizes
an antagonistic or mature character, or steps out of the fiction to discuss the
AI, prompt, rules, or guidelines.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** Character-fidelity research evaluates whether speech, action, and
inner thought match the prescribed persona; villain-roleplay research also
finds systematic quality loss as characters become less prosocial. Persistent
persona studies show that this fidelity fades in long interactions.

**Dimension: Scene continuity without puppeteering the user**

**Good (domain expert would accept):** The reply picks up the selected branch's
exact location, events, relationships, unresolved action, and last utterance.
The character may change mood or judgment in a way earned by the history, while
leaving {{user}}'s dialogue, thoughts, decisions, and consequential actions for
the user to choose.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The reply resets or contradicts the scene,
revives a stale swipe branch, robotically repeats static traits despite events,
narrates the user's internal state or dialogue, or resolves a choice on the
user's behalf.

**Stakes:** High

**Source:** Dynamic-persona research distinguishes stable identity from
history-dependent emotional adaptation; extended-dialogue research identifies
persona drift as a long-context failure. Preserving player agency is an
established interactive-roleplay practice and is locked by this phase's
character-next-reply contract.

**Dimension: Fictional follow-through and refusal precision**

**Good (domain expert would accept):** Mature, explicit, dark, violent, taboo,
profane, controversial, antagonistic, or otherwise morally complex material
continues as the selected private fiction calls for. Only an explicit generic
assistant/guideline refusal is intercepted; ordinary character dialogue such
as “I can't go with you” reaches the user unchanged.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** A generic apology, safety-guideline
explanation, warning, redirect, or refusal is displayed, spoken, or persisted;
the role is flattened toward generic prosocial behavior; or benign in-character
negation is falsely suppressed and retried.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** Persona prompting and model choice measurably affect false-refusal
rates, while villain-roleplay evaluations find alignment bias suppresses
authentic negative traits. RayMe REQ-37 and REQ-39 make unfiltered default
fiction and precise bounded recovery the product contract.

**Dimension: Live conversational immediacy**

**Good (domain expert would accept):** RayMe holds only a small bounded prefix,
then releases accepted or recovered text to captions and TTS. First speech
starts while the accepted LLM and TTS streams are still open; explicit
Interrupt stops late chunks and the call returns to a truthful controllable
state.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** Refusal handling waits for a whole response,
creates a conspicuous dead-air gap, lets rejected text reach captions or
speech, allows late audio after Interrupt, or strands the call in a false
Listening/Thinking state.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** RayMe's LIVE-CALL-INVARIANTS and REQ-39/44/45; the product is a live
phone-call simulator, so early playback and interruption behavior are
acceptance criteria, not background implementation details.

**Dimension: Inspectable request truth**

**Good (domain expert would accept):** For Send, Regenerate, Swipe, Continue,
preview, call offer, and call turn, the inspector shows the exact ordered
roles/content, global-versus-card source, override expansion, context drops,
model profile, and sampler fields actually sent. It omits credentials; refusal
logs expose attempt, reason, prefix length, and timing without storing prompt
or rejected prose.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** Preview differs from the wire request, text
and calls use different prompt logic, a hidden fallback changes mode or content,
debug evidence cannot explain a failure, or credentials/private rejected text
appear in diagnostics.

**Stakes:** High

**Source:** RayMe REQ-38 and phase decisions D-09, D-22, and D-24; experienced
roleplay users need to see which card, global prompt, history, and generation
controls actually shaped a turn.

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

1. **Persona flattening and out-of-character leakage:** distinctive motives,
   negative traits, and voice collapse into a generic prosocial assistant,
   often with policy, prompt, or AI meta-commentary.
2. **Long-thread drift, branch amnesia, and emotional rigidity:** the model
   forgets the current location or selected alternate, contradicts established
   relationships, or repeats a trait mechanically instead of adapting to what
   happened in the scene.
3. **Agency theft:** the character writes the user's speech, thoughts, consent,
   decision, or decisive action, closing off the user's intended contribution
   to the collaborative scene.
4. **Refusal/recovery coupling failure:** a generic refusal escapes into speech
   and durable history, benign in-world negation is misclassified, or the
   prefix guard/request-path divergence hides the failure by turning a live
   call into whole-response generate-then-play.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

No sector-specific regulated decision context or domain-specific content-
moderation mandate was identified for this private fictional-roleplay
deployment. Adding an NSFW filter or hidden moderation layer would contradict
the locked product scope rather than satisfy a discovered domain requirement.

For EU distribution, AI Act Article 50(1) applies from 2 August 2026 and
requires providers of systems that interact directly with people to make the
AI interaction apparent unless it is already obvious. RayMe is overtly an AI
character/call simulator, and that framing must remain clear. Article 2(10)
excludes deployer obligations for a natural person's purely personal,
non-professional use; provider obligations should be re-checked before public
release or professional use.

The local/offline posture does not justify secret-bearing diagnostics. Existing
product privacy constraints remain directly relevant: the effective-request
inspector excludes API keys, refusal telemetry is metadata-only, and private
prompt, history, and call content is not exported for evaluation by default.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Official OpenAI Python SDK — direct Chat Completions; no orchestration framework

**Version:** `openai==2.32.0` (existing RayMe pin)

**Rationale:**
Phase 09.1 is a deterministic prompt-composition and streaming-control layer,
not an agent workflow. Keeping RayMe's pinned direct SDK preserves exact request
inspection, local Qwen/llama-server compatibility, and the shortest path from
accepted tokens to live-call playback. The bounded refusal guard belongs at the
existing shared iterator boundary, where it can protect both streamed and
collected completions without introducing a framework state machine.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| LangChain Core 1.x | Adds abstraction and latency without helping the direct OpenAI-compatible prompt/stream contract; reconsider only if provider-specific serialization grows beyond the phase scope. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** No — the client contract stays model-agnostic and OpenAI-compatible.

### Entry Point Pattern

```python
import secrets
from collections.abc import AsyncIterator, Sequence

import httpx
from openai import AsyncOpenAI
from openai.types.chat import ChatCompletionMessageParam


async def iter_qwen_chat_tokens(
    *,
    base_url: str,
    api_key: str | None,
    model: str,
    messages: Sequence[ChatCompletionMessageParam],
) -> AsyncIterator[str]:
    """Minimal direct-SDK stream for the primary llama-server profile."""

    timeout = httpx.Timeout(90.0, connect=5.0, read=30.0, write=10.0, pool=5.0)
    async with AsyncOpenAI(
        base_url=base_url,
        api_key=api_key or "local-no-key",
        timeout=timeout,
        max_retries=0,  # RayMe owns bounded retries and cancellation.
    ) as client:
        stream = await client.chat.completions.create(
            model=model,
            messages=messages,
            stream=True,
            max_tokens=512,
            temperature=0.80,
            top_p=0.95,
            presence_penalty=0.0,
            frequency_penalty=0.0,
            seed=secrets.randbelow(2**31),
            extra_body={
                # llama-server fields not typed as standard OpenAI parameters.
                "top_k": 40,
                "min_p": 0.05,
                "repeat_penalty": 1.05,
                "chat_template_kwargs": {"enable_thinking": False},
            },
        )
        # The context manager closes the HTTP response on rejection/cancel too.
        async with stream:
            async for chunk in stream:
                if not chunk.choices:
                    continue
                text = chunk.choices[0].delta.content
                if isinstance(text, str) and text:
                    yield text
```

Production code should reuse one lifespan-managed `AsyncOpenAI` client and put
RayMe's bounded prefix guard between the chunk iterator and every downstream
consumer. The generic OpenAI-compatible profile uses the same pattern but
omits unproven llama-server-only fields from `extra_body`.

### Key Abstractions

| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `AsyncOpenAI` | Async HTTP client whose `base_url` can target OpenAI or a compatible local server. | Reuse it from FastAPI lifespan state for all text and call completions. |
| `chat.completions.create(..., stream=True)` | Starts a Chat Completions request and returns an `AsyncStream` of `ChatCompletionChunk` values. | Use for every user-visible roleplay turn so accepted text can reach captions/TTS early. |
| `choices[0].delta.content` | The incremental text field in each streamed chunk; some chunks have no choice or no content. | Normalize it in the lowest shared token iterator and ignore empty/control chunks. |
| Standard request parameters | Typed fields such as `messages`, `model`, `max_tokens`, `temperature`, `top_p`, penalties, `seed`, and `stream`. | Keep them in the exact inspectable request contract shared by text and calls. |
| `extra_body` | SDK escape hatch that merges provider-specific top-level JSON into the request. | Send llama-server `top_k`, `min_p`, `repeat_penalty`, and `chat_template_kwargs`; omit unsupported extras for the generic profile. |

### Common Pitfalls

1. **Leaving SDK retries and timeouts at their defaults.** SDK 2.32.0 retries
   selected failures twice and otherwise permits a ten-minute timeout. Hidden
   retries reuse one request/seed, multiply live-call delay, and make the
   phase's three semantic attempts unauditable. Configure `max_retries=0` and
   explicit connect/read/write/pool timeouts at this boundary.
2. **Abandoning a rejected or cancelled stream without closing it.** A refusal
   retry, Interrupt, hangup, or task cancellation must exit an async context
   manager (or explicitly `await stream.close()`), otherwise the HTTP response
   and llama-server slot can remain occupied.
3. **Treating “OpenAI-compatible” as identical to OpenAI.** llama.cpp explicitly
   makes no full-compatibility guarantee and requires a suitable chat template.
   Resolve a visible model profile, test its exact payload, and never send
   provider extras through the generic profile by assumption.
4. **Replacing rather than merging `extra_body`.** Adding Qwen
   `chat_template_kwargs` must not erase `min_p`, `top_k`, or
   `repeat_penalty`, and adding samplers must not erase
   `enable_thinking=False`. Merge nested dictionaries deliberately and cover
   the exact wire payload with tests.
5. **Using whole-response parsing on live dialogue.** `chat.completions.parse()`
   is useful for bounded non-streaming structured work, but JSON/schema
   accumulation is the wrong contract for spoken prose. A live turn may hold
   only the bounded refusal prefix; safe text must release while the upstream
   LLM and TTS streams remain open.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**

The primary acceptance profile is the configured OMEN Qwen model served by
llama-server (`unsloth/Qwen3.5-27B` where the deployment reports that exact
model ID). Keep the ID in Settings rather than source code, show the resolved
profile in the inspector, and record the server-reported model ID during
deployment verification. `auto` may recognize a known Qwen ID, but users must
be able to select `qwen_llama_server` or `generic_openai_compatible`
explicitly.

| Field | Roleplay Default | Contract |
|-------|------------------|----------|
| Context limit | 16,384 estimated tokens | Bounded/editable and clamped to the deployed server capacity; never infer capacity from a model marketing value. |
| `max_tokens` | 512 | Always explicit; reserve it before selecting history. Use the broadly compatible Chat Completions field rather than switching the local path to `max_completion_tokens`. |
| `temperature` | 0.80 | Editable within a validated range; send as a standard SDK field. |
| `top_p` / `top_k` / `min_p` | `0.95` / `40` / `0.05` | `top_p` is standard; the Qwen/llama-server profile sends `top_k` and `min_p` through `extra_body`. |
| Repetition / presence / frequency penalty | `1.05` / `0.0` / `0.0` | Map the UI's repetition penalty to llama-server's wire field `repeat_penalty`; omit it from the generic profile unless capability-tested. |
| Seed | Fresh random integer in `[0, 2**31 - 1]` per attempt | Preview says “generated at send time”; retries never reuse the rejected attempt's seed. |
| Qwen no-thinking | Enabled | Preserve both the copied outbound last-user `/no_think` suffix and merged `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking=false`; never mutate persisted history. |

Assistant and Custom modes share the same bounded request schema. Changing the
prompt mode must not silently change the model profile or samplers.

**Core Pattern:**

One composer creates a typed build result. One model adapter turns it into
request kwargs. One lowest-level iterator owns the SDK stream and the bounded
refusal attempts. Text SSE, collected completions, call captions, and TTS may
consume only accepted tokens from that iterator.

```python
MAX_ATTEMPTS = 3


async def stream_accepted_turn(client, composer, adapter, settings):
    """Production shape; named helper types are RayMe domain abstractions."""

    for attempt in range(1, MAX_ATTEMPTS + 1):
        # Retry correction is late, short, and never contains the refusal prose.
        correction = None if attempt == 1 else settings.retry_correction
        built = composer.build(retry_correction=correction)
        request = adapter.request_kwargs(
            built=built,
            settings=settings,
            seed=secrets.randbelow(2**31),  # fresh for every semantic attempt
            stream=True,
        )
        guard = PrefixRefusalGuard(
            max_characters=settings.refusal_prefix_characters,
            max_estimated_tokens=settings.refusal_prefix_tokens,
        )
        rejected = False

        stream = await client.chat.completions.create(**request)
        # Exiting this block closes an explicitly rejected/cancelled stream.
        async with stream:
            async for chunk in stream:
                text = chunk_text(chunk)
                if not text:
                    continue
                decision = guard.feed(text)
                if decision.refused:
                    rejected = True
                    break
                for accepted_text in decision.released_text:
                    yield accepted_text

            if not rejected:
                final = guard.finish()
                rejected = final.refused
                for accepted_text in final.released_text:
                    yield accepted_text

        if not rejected:
            return

        record_refusal_attempt(
            attempt=attempt,
            reason_code=guard.reason_code,
            prefix_length=guard.prefix_length,
            time_to_decision_ms=guard.time_to_decision_ms,
        )

    # This error contains no prompt or rejected prose and persists/speaks nothing.
    raise LLMRefusalExhausted(code="llm_refusal_exhausted")
```

`PrefixRefusalGuard` can reject only before release. It releases immediately on
a clearly safe completed sentence or the hard prefix ceiling, even if the
upstream stream remains open. After any accepted text is yielded, later text is
never invisibly replaced; a later transport failure is surfaced as a stream
failure rather than starting a hidden new answer.

**Tool Use:**

No model tools are required: omit `tools`, `tool_choice`, and function-calling
machinery. Prompt composition, macro expansion, budgeting, refusal detection,
and retry selection are deterministic server-side code, not LLM tool calls.
This keeps the local path independent of llama-server's template-dependent
function calling. Existing Pydantic models validate settings/preview payloads,
and existing pytest layers provide eval coverage; no vector store, embedding
model, or hosted tracing dependency belongs in Phase 09.1.

**State Management:**

- Persist global mode, prompt text, bounds, samplers, and model profile in the
  existing `app_settings` JSON snapshot. These values resolve live for old and
  new threads.
- Keep character identity, card Main/post-history fields, and the new
  `character_snapshot_mes_example` truthful to thread creation. Existing null
  example snapshots stay null; never backfill from a later card edit.
- Treat `PromptBuildResult`, adapter kwargs, prefix buffers, attempt seeds, and
  retry corrections as request-scoped state. Preview may expose exact messages
  and non-secret fields, but never the API key or a pre-generated runtime seed.
- Persist assistant text only after the accepted stream completes. In calls,
  persist once after accepted speech completes. Rejected prefixes and attempts
  never reach SSE, `ai_token`, TTS, logs, or storage.
- Carry the existing turn/cancellation identity through every attempt. Explicit
  Interrupt or hangup cancels the current SDK stream and makes all late LLM/TTS
  chunks ineligible for normal completion.

**Context Window Strategy:**

Compute `input_budget = configured_context_limit - max_tokens - safety_margin`,
with a conservative margin such as `max(256, ceil(context_limit * 0.05))`.
Token estimates must be deterministic and visibly labeled estimates. Always
keep resolved Main, persona, Auxiliary, late post-history, and the newest user
turn. If those mandatory sections alone do not fit, return a typed
`prompt_budget_exceeded` error rather than silently truncating them.

Add complete example groups and selected non-stale history in the locked
order, then enforce the budget by removing oldest real history first and
oldest complete example groups second. Never leave an orphaned example turn.
Continue must end with the assistant prefill after the late instruction, and
call offers must also honor the AI-backend's 48-message ceiling. Phase 09.1
does not add hidden LLM summarization: exact omissions and counts remain visible
in the inspector.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Written by `gsd-ai-researcher`. Cross-cutting patterns every developer building AI systems needs — independent of framework choice.

### Structured Outputs with Pydantic

```python
from typing import Literal

from pydantic import BaseModel, ConfigDict, Field, field_validator


class AcceptedRoleplayTurn(BaseModel):
    """Validated persistence envelope around accepted streamed prose."""

    model_config = ConfigDict(extra="forbid", frozen=True)

    text: str
    mode: Literal["roleplay", "assistant", "custom"]
    attempt: int = Field(ge=1, le=3)
    seed: int = Field(ge=0, le=2**31 - 1)

    @field_validator("text")
    @classmethod
    def text_must_not_be_blank(cls, value: str) -> str:
        if not value.strip():
            raise ValueError("accepted turn must contain non-whitespace text")
        return value
```

The normal RayMe dialogue is intentionally free-form streamed text, so do not
force it through `response_format`: a JSON envelope would delay speech and risk
speaking serialization syntax. Pydantic validates the deterministic composed
request/preview before the SDK call and validates the accepted persistence
envelope after stream completion. The bounded prefix guard is the semantic
validator for the LLM text: original plus at most two retries, each with a
fresh seed and late correction.

SDK 2.32.0 does provide
`await client.chat.completions.parse(..., response_format=SomePydanticModel)`;
it converts the model to JSON Schema and returns `message.parsed`. llama-server
also documents schema-constrained `response_format`, but the generic
OpenAI-compatible contract does not guarantee it. Use `.parse()` only for a
future non-live structured control-plane task after a capability test, never
for live roleplay. Allow at most two schema-repair retries, log only request ID,
attempt, finish reason, and Pydantic error locations, then surface a typed
`invalid_model_output`. A Pydantic failure in RayMe's deterministic composer is
an implementation error and gets no LLM retry.

### Async-First Design

Use `AsyncOpenAI` from FastAPI's existing event loop and reuse a lifespan-owned
client/connection pool. Await creation of the Chat Completions stream, then
`async for` its chunks. Do not call `asyncio.run()` inside FastAPI, do not use
the synchronous `OpenAI` client in an async route, and do not swallow task
cancellation. Every refusal, exception, Interrupt, and hangup path must close
the active stream and re-raise cancellation so downstream TTS also stops.

Stream user-visible prose; await non-streaming structured results only when the
whole validated object is required before use. The refusal guard is not a
reason to collect a turn: it holds only its fixed prefix and must prove in tests
that accepted tokens and first speech release while the LLM stream is still
open.

### Prompt Engineering Discipline

Keep stable instructions and card persona in named composer sections; keep real
user utterances as `user` messages and assistant history as `assistant`
messages. Keep the late post-history instruction as its own source-tagged
section even when a model profile must serialize roles differently. Never
concatenate a real user turn into a system string, and render all prompt/card
text as escaped text in the inspector.

Card examples are the phase's few-shot examples. Parse them once into complete
`<START>` groups, preserve speaker roles, insert them before real history, and
drop only whole groups. There is no dynamic example retrieval or RAG in this
phase. Expand `{{original}}` first, then `{{char}}` and `{{user}}`, once and
nonrecursively; leave unknown macros visible.

Apply Qwen `/no_think` only to a copied outbound last-user message and merge the
template flag in the adapter. Never save the suffix into conversation history.
Set `max_tokens` on every request and reserve it during composition; production
must never rely on an endpoint's unbounded/default output length. Version the
built-in prompts and include section IDs, override source, and exact transmitted
content in preview tests.

### Context Window Management

Use the deterministic budget and drop order from Section 4 for text, message
actions, preview, and calls. A model-advertised context length is not evidence
of the running llama-server slot size; use the configured verified capacity.
The llama-server-specific input-token endpoint may be used by deployment
diagnostics, but the generic path must work with the conservative estimator.

Do not introduce invisible conversational summarization in Phase 09.1. It can
erase roleplay details, hide what the model actually saw, and add another model
call before first audio. If a later phase adds summaries, store them as an
explicit source-tagged synthetic section with coverage tests and keep recent
verbatim turns; never silently overwrite durable history.

### Cost and Latency Budget

The primary offline llama-server path has **$0 provider-token charge per turn**;
hardware electricity and amortization are operational costs not yet measured.
Budget a representative turn as at most 4,000 input plus 512 output tokens. At
10,000 turns/month that is 40 million input and 5.12 million output tokens. For
a remote compatible provider, calculate rather than hard-code volatile prices:
`monthly_cost = 40 * input_price_per_million + 5.12 * output_price_per_million`,
and show the configured estimate separately from local cost.

Bound refusal work by the prefix ceiling. A rejected attempt must stop there,
so the worst semantic path is one accepted turn plus at most two short rejected
prefixes, not three full generations. Track composer time, upstream time to
first token, guard time to release, retry count, first speech, and completion
separately. The guard's CPU work should remain below 5 ms p95, and acceptance
must prove release at a safe sentence or hard prefix ceiling before upstream
completion rather than asserting a misleading whole-turn average.

Do not cache generated dialogue: exact-match caching defeats fresh seeds and
semantic caching can replay the wrong character state or private turn. An
exact-match cache is safe only for immutable prompt-template parsing or token
estimates keyed by content hash and prompt version; semantic response caching
stays disabled. There are no model-routed subtasks in this phase—the
deterministic classifier, composer, and budgeter are both cheaper and faster
than another LLM. Any later summarizer/router must use a separately configured
smaller local model and its own eval and latency budget.

## 5. Evaluation Strategy

### Dimensions

| Dimension | Rubric (Pass/Fail or 1-5) | Measurement Approach | Priority |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------------------|----------|
| Character embodiment and in-world voice (tone/style) | **PASS:** The turn is recognizably the selected character, continues only the fiction in its established diction and emotional posture, and contains no AI identity, prompt explanation, policy reference, generic safety warning, or editorial preamble. **FAIL:** The voice is generic-helpful, prosocially flattened, contradictory to a durable card trait, or steps out of the scene. | **Human** is authoritative on the eight fixed fidelity cases. A local **LLM Judge** may assist only after calibration described below; deterministic meta/refusal phrases are also checked by **Code**. | Critical |
| Scene continuity and user agency | **PASS:** The request and reply use only the selected, non-stale branch, preserve current location/relationships/unresolved action, and leave the user's speech, thoughts, decisions, and consequential actions to the user. **FAIL:** A stale swipe or dropped required fact is revived, the scene resets, or the character puppeteers the user. | **Code** proves branch/context selection and complete example-group drops; **Human** scores semantic continuity and agency. A calibrated local **LLM Judge** is advisory. | High |
| Fictional follow-through and refusal precision | **PASS:** Mature, dark, explicit, profane, antagonistic, or controversial private fiction continues when requested; every explicit generic assistant/guideline refusal is caught before release, while benign in-character negation such as “I can't go with you” passes byte-for-byte without retry. **FAIL:** Any generic refusal is emitted, spoken, or persisted, or any labeled benign negation is suppressed. | **Code** over the frozen refusal/negation corpus, arbitrary token-boundary expansions, downstream event spies, and the deployed Qwen matrix. No LLM judge decides this gate. | Critical |
| Shared request composition and instruction following | **PASS:** Send, Regenerate, Swipe, Continue, preview, call offer, and call turn use the same composer and match the gold ordered roles/content, section sources, override/macro expansion, context drops, model profile, and sampler payload exactly. Continue retains its final assistant prefill and call offers respect the 48-message ceiling. **FAIL:** Any path has an unrecorded message or field difference, hidden fallback, stale global prompt, partial example group, mutated persisted `/no_think` suffix, or second prompt algorithm. | **Code** with injected fixed seeds, captured SDK kwargs, golden request fixtures, and API/Playwright contract tests. | Critical |
| Live conversational immediacy | **PASS:** Accepted text releases at the first clearly safe completed sentence or hard prefix ceiling while the upstream LLM stream is still open; on retry, first caption and first speech occur while the accepted LLM and TTS streams are still open. **FAIL:** A whole response is collected, first speech follows stream completion, rejected text reaches a downstream consumer, VoxCPM2 whole-synthesis fallback is called, or immediate and final timing fields are conflated. | **Code** with deliberately slow async streams and event ordering assertions; **Playwright** against the deployed call-shaped path. | Critical |

| Failure and escalation accuracy | **PASS:** The original plus at most two bounded retries use distinct seeds and metadata-only reason records; after three explicit refusals RayMe returns sanitized `llm_refusal_exhausted`, without canned prose or mode changes. | Interaction safety: cancellation, state truth, and secret containment | **PASS:** Interrupt/hangup closes active LLM and TTS work, rejects every late chunk, and leaves a truthful controllable call state. Inspector output is non-mutating, HTML-escaped, and contains no API key, bearer token, authorization header, or runtime seed; logs/evidence contain neither prompt/history nor rejected prose. **FAIL:** Any late normal completion/audio/persistence event occurs, the UI is stranded in a false state, diagnostics execute markup, or a seeded secret canary escapes. Mature fictional content is not a safety failure in this product contract. | **Code** using cancellation barriers, event/database spies, recursive secret-canary scans, and Playwright state/escaping tests. | Critical |

### Blocking Acceptance Gates

All Critical gates are conjunctive: averages cannot hide one escape, parity
diff, late chunk, or secret leak. The evidence verifier must report every gate
ID and fail closed when an artifact, expected commit, model identity, or timing
field is missing.

| Gate | Strict acceptance threshold |
|------|-----------------------------|
| `REF-01` classifier precision/recall | **100% recall** on all 10 labeled generic/guideline refusals and **100% specificity** on all 12 labeled benign/non-refusal prefixes, under every canonical fragmentation. Downstream emitted refusal bytes and false retries are both exactly **0**. |
| `REF-02` bounded recovery | Exactly 1–3 attempts; no more than 2 retries; a distinct injected seed per attempt; rejected stream closed before the next request; rejected prose absent from retry messages, logs, events, and storage. Exhaustion produces only `llm_refusal_exhausted`. |
| `PROMPT-01` exact composition parity | **0 field or ordering diffs** against gold fixtures for every action/path. With a fixed injected seed, captured wire kwargs equal the expected model-profile payload exactly; preview equals the same build result for all inspectable fields and labels the runtime seed “generated at send time.” Credential-canary matches are **0**. |
| `STREAM-01` bounded early release | In **100%** of deliberately slow safe streams, first accepted emission occurs before upstream completion and no later than the configured character/token ceiling (or earlier safe-sentence decision). Guard CPU time is **<5 ms p95** over at least 100 corpus feeds. No implementation may require whole-response accumulation. |
| `CALL-01` live first speech | In every controlled slow accepted and refusal-then-accepted case, `first_caption < llm_complete` and `ai_audio_started < llm_complete` and `ai_audio_started < tts_stream_complete`. VoxCPM2 whole-synthesis fallback calls are **0**. Immediate events contain **0** final-only timing values. |
| `STATE-01` persistence and cancellation | Exactly one canonical assistant row/audio reference for a successful completed turn; exactly zero for rejected, exhausted, pre-release failed, interrupted, or hung-up turns. After Interrupt, late caption/audio/normal-completion events and durable writes are exactly **0**, and active LLM/TTS streams are observed closed. |
| `QUALITY-01` domain review | Across the eight fixed roleplay cases, no output has AI/policy meta-commentary, an explicit generic refusal, or user-agency theft; at least **7/8** score 4 or 5 for both embodiment and continuity, with mean **≥4.0/5** in each dimension. Each mature/antagonistic case must preserve its defining non-prosocial traits. |
| `DEPLOY-01` real Qwen corpus | On the exact deployed commit and server-reported primary Qwen model, the 12-scenario blank/weak/strong-card subset runs at 3 recorded fixed seeds (**36 turns**): emitted generic/guideline refusals **0**, persisted rejected prefixes **0**, false retries on labeled benign negation **0**, and request/inspector diffs **0**. |
| `PRIV-01` evidence privacy | Recursive seeded-canary scan over inspector responses, structured logs, Playwright attachments, and committed `results/` artifacts finds **0 credentials/authorization headers**. Logs and saved evidence contain **0 raw prompts, histories, or rejected prose**; only explicitly local inspector responses may contain escaped prompt/history content. |

### Reference Dataset

**Size:** **44 canonical labeled records** before production handoff, expanded
deterministically into at least 176 classifier stream cases plus request-path,
lifecycle, and deployed generations. The corpus starts during implementation;
IDs and expected labels are frozen before classifier tuning. Every newly found
production failure is added as a failing record before its fix.

**Composition:**

- **10 explicit-refusal positives:** includes the exact observed “I cannot
  continue this conversation” plus safety-guideline behavior, apology + policy,
  assistant-identity, warning, and redirect variants. Each is replayed as one
  chunk, one-character chunks, phrase-boundary chunks, punctuation-boundary
  chunks, and four deterministic irregular fragmentations.
- **12 benign/non-refusal negatives:** in-character “I can't go with you,” firm
  “no,” threats, profanity, an in-world apology, discussion of fictional rules
  or safety, quoted refusal language, and mature/antagonistic continuation.
  Every case must be emitted byte-for-byte once with zero retry under the same
  fragmentations.
- **8 exact-composition gold cases:** blank, weak, strong, mature, and
  antagonistic cards; blank inheritance; `{{original}}` override order;
  nonrecursive/unknown macros; complete `<START>` example groups; stale swipe
  branches; budget drops; Continue prefill; Qwen and generic profiles. Together
  they exercise Send, Regenerate, Swipe, Continue, preview, call offer, and call
  turn with captured request kwargs.
- **6 stream/lifecycle traces:** early safe sentence, hard-ceiling release,
  refusal then accepted retry, three-refusal exhaustion, post-release transport
  failure, and Interrupt after first audio. Slow iterators expose explicit
  `llm_open`, `llm_complete`, `tts_open`, `tts_complete`, caption, speech,
  cancellation, close, and persistence events.
- **8 domain-quality conversations:** distinctive voice, mature fiction,
  antagonistic character, dynamic emotional change, long selected branch,
  edited/stale downstream branch, continuity across text-to-call, and a user
  choice that tempts agency theft.
- **Deployed subset:** 12 representative blank/weak/strong-card generation
  scenarios, including benign negation and mature/antagonistic fiction, each at
  three recorded fixed seeds on the configured Qwen/llama-server profile. A
  controlled slow OpenAI-compatible fixture on the deployed stack separately
  forces refusal-attempt-1/accept-attempt-2 so call retry ordering is observable
  rather than dependent on Qwen choosing to refuse.

**Labeling:**

- The product owner/experienced roleplay user signs off explicit-refusal versus
  benign-negation labels and the unfiltered-fiction expectation.
- A character-card author or interactive-fiction editor scores embodiment,
  continuity, adaptation, and user agency on the locked 1–5 rubric. The
  live-call evaluator signs the lifecycle event order, and the prompt/privacy
  maintainer signs the gold request and redaction fixtures.
- Code-derived labels own exact roles/content, source/override metadata,
  payloads, attempts, seeds, byte emission, event order, persistence counts,
  and secret-canary results. These do not use an LLM judge.
- Any local LLM judge is advisory until calibrated on at least 20 independently
  human-scored outputs with Spearman correlation **≥0.70** and **zero critical
  false-passes** for refusal, AI/policy leakage, or agency theft. If calibration
  falls below either threshold, human labels remain authoritative and the judge
  cannot block or waive release.

## 6. Guardrails

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Bounded prefix refusal guard | Before any accepted byte is released, combined evidence shows a refusal verb plus generic assistant identity, policy/guideline/safety language, apology, warning, or redirect. A single phrase alone is insufficient. | **Block that attempt:** emit nothing, close its SDK stream, record metadata only, and retry with a fresh seed and short late correction. After attempt 3, return sanitized `llm_refusal_exhausted`; speak/persist nothing. |
| Pre-release commit barrier | A stream is refused, exhausted, cancelled, or fails before accepted release; a call has not completed accepted speech. | **Block persistence and audio commit:** no assistant row, saved AI audio, normal completion event, or retry prose. Persist exactly once only after the accepted completion contract is satisfied. |
| Turn ownership and explicit cancellation barrier | Interrupt, hangup, task cancellation, or a chunk/event whose call/session/turn identity is no longer current. | **Cancel and discard:** close active LLM/TTS work, reject all late chunks/events, re-raise task cancellation, and drive the UI to the truthful surviving state. Automatic microphone/VAD barge-in remains disabled. |
| Request/budget/profile validation | Typed settings are invalid, mandatory prompt sections exceed budget, the model profile cannot serialize a required field, or an SDK payload differs from the validated build result. | **Block the request:** return a typed sanitized error (`prompt_budget_exceeded`, invalid settings/profile, or transport error). Never silently truncate mandatory sections, change mode/profile, or remove sampler/template fields. |
| Inspector and diagnostic allowlist | Inspector serialization or log/evidence fields contain credential keys, authorization headers, runtime seeds, raw rejected prose, or unescaped display content. | **Block/redact the diagnostic output and fail the security test/evidence gate.** Inspector remains non-mutating and local; metadata logs never include prompt/history. |

These are deterministic, request-local checks. Do **not** add an online LLM
judge, hosted moderation call, generic NSFW filter, or whole-response safety
scan: each would add latency and the content filter would contradict the locked
private-roleplay contract. The only held model text is the bounded refusal
prefix.

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** RayMe's existing local structured application logs plus the
sanitized Phase 09.1 JSON evidence bundle are the production default. There is
**no mandatory tracing service, account, network exporter, or new production
runtime dependency**. Use stable event names and an explicit field allowlist:
`call_id`/`session_id`/`turn_id` where available, action, prompt-contract
version, request-shape version, mode, model profile, attempt, classifier reason
code, prefix character/estimated-token counts, retry count, first-token and
release timings, call timing milestones, cancellation/close state, terminal
outcome, and drop counts. Never log API keys, authorization headers, runtime
seed values, prompt/card/history content, generated/rejected prose, or audio.

**Optional local trace viewer:** Arize Phoenix may be launched self-hosted on a
developer machine for a difficult timing investigation. It is non-gating,
offline/local-only, is not added to RayMe's production dependencies, and may
receive only the same allowlisted metadata. No hosted Phoenix endpoint or
remote OTLP exporter is permitted by default.

# Optional developer environment only:

**Key Metrics to Track:**

| Metric family | Required fields / interpretation |
|---------------|----------------------------------|
| Refusal decisions | `attempt`, `reason_code`, prefix characters/estimated tokens, retry count, time to decision/release, exhausted count, and terminal outcome. Keep rejected prose absent. |
| Prompt/request contract | Prompt-contract and request-shape versions, action, mode, model profile/server-reported model ID, estimated input tokens, dropped history/example-group counts, and typed budget/profile failures. Exact content is visible only through the local on-demand inspector. |
| Streaming latency and ordering | Composer time, upstream time to first token, guard CPU and wall time to first accepted release, first caption, first speech/`ai_audio_started`, LLM completion, TTS stream completion, final playout, inter-chunk gaps, and explicit booleans for release/speech-before-completion. Immediate and final metrics remain separate. |
| Lifecycle and persistence | Attempt stream opened/closed, current turn identity, Interrupt/hangup/cancel, late chunks discarded, normal completion count, assistant rows/audio references committed, and typed terminal failure code. |
| Privacy/evidence integrity | Inspector credential-redaction result, secret-canary count, evidence schema version, expected/running commit, artifact freshness, model identity, and per-gate pass/fail. No raw content. |

**Alert Thresholds:**

- **Immediate Critical / block handoff:** emitted generic refusal, false benign
  retry in the frozen corpus, request/preview diff, credential canary, rejected
  or cancelled persistence/speech, late normal event after Interrupt,
  release/first-speech ordering failure, whole-response fallback, fourth
  attempt, missing stream close, or expected-commit/model mismatch is greater
  than **0**.
- **Immediate visible failure + review:** any `llm_refusal_exhausted` event. It
  remains a truthful typed user-visible error, not a reason to emit canned
  dialogue or change modes.
- **Performance regression:** guard CPU **≥5 ms p95** over at least 100 feeds
  blocks release. End-to-end first speech **>800 ms p95** over at least 20
  successful turns or a **>25%** p95 increase from the last accepted deployed
  evidence triggers investigation; only ordering/streaming violations are hard
  release failures because REQ-46 defines 800 ms as a target.
- **Quality drift:** retry rate above **5% over 20 turns**, three consecutive
  retries, any context-budget failure, or any user-reported out-of-character or
  agency-theft turn enters the next local review batch. This is an investigation
  signal, not permission to broaden refusal detection or add moderation.
- **Evidence invalid:** missing required artifact/gate, non-allowlisted result,
  stale artifact, or verifier/schema failure blocks deployment acceptance.

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**

- Record allowlisted metadata counters for **100%** of local interactions; do
  not sample or export prose, prompt history, credentials, or audio into
  telemetry.
- Review **100%** of retries, exhaustions, typed budget/profile errors,
  Interrupts/hangups with late chunks, persistence mismatches, model-profile
  resolution changes, and deployed gate failures.
- For semantic local review, select at least **10% or 5 turns (whichever is
  larger) per release candidate** from existing locally persisted threads,
  weighted toward mature/antagonistic cards, threads with context drops,
  long/edited/swiped branches, text-to-call transitions, benign negation, and
  unusually long guard holds. Review is opt-in and local; it creates only
  rubric scores/scenario IDs in evidence, not copied dialogue.
- Retain the complete 44-record canonical corpus and the latest passing
  commit/model-identified deployed evidence. Expand from real failures; never
  replace or relabel a regression solely to restore a passing score.

**Warnings:** Evaluation must not reintroduce automatic microphone/VAD barge-in,
wait for whole LLM/TTS completion, use a hosted judge or trace exporter, treat
mature fictional content as a violation, or let an aggregate quality score
waive an exact Critical gate. Phoenix and any future Promptfoo configuration
remain optional developer aids; pytest/Playwright, the fixed corpus, and
commit-identified deployed evidence are authoritative.
