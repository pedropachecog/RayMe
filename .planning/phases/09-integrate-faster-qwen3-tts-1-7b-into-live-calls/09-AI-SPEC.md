## 1. System Classification

**System Type:** Conversational speech generation

**Description:**
RayMe turns streaming assistant text into a cloned voice during a full-duplex simulated phone call. The builder selects a saved voice made from a short reference recording and matching transcript; the self-hosted OMEN backend must start natural, intelligible audio early, keep supplying it faster than playback, remain stable over long conversations, and stop promptly when the caller interrupts.

**Critical Failure Modes:**
1. The voice progressively becomes muffled, whisper-like, noisy, silent, or unintelligible over repeated turns.
2. Playback waits for full response/TTS completion, starves during live playout, or accumulates an unbounded audio backlog.
3. A mismatched reference transcript causes repetition, token-cap runaway, implausibly long output, or corrupted cloning.
4. Barge-in/hangup fails to stop generation and late audio or normal completion leaks into the next call state.
5. CUDA/model failure crashes the AI backend, triggers CPU fallback, exceeds RTX 3060 VRAM, or leaves hidden loading/prewarm stalls.

## 1b. Domain Context

> Researched by `gsd-domain-researcher`. Grounds the evaluation strategy in domain expert knowledge.

**Industry Vertical:** Self-hosted consumer conversational speech and zero-shot voice cloning

**User Population:** The RayMe builder/product owner who creates and tests saved voices; the people whose recordings and vocal identity are used as references; and listeners participating in simulated live calls.

**Stakes Level:** High. This is not a safety-critical or regulated-industry deployment, but failure is immediately audible in a live conversation and misuse of an identifiable person's voice can create privacy, consent, impersonation, and reputational harm.

**Output Consequence:** Assistant text is spoken immediately into a full-duplex call in a recognizable cloned voice. Quality failures break comprehension and turn-taking in real time; identity or provenance failures can falsely represent the reference speaker.

### What Domain Experts Evaluate Against

#### Dimension: Voice likeness and identity stability

**Good (domain expert would accept):** In blinded reference-versus-clone listening, the product owner consistently hears the intended speaker rather than merely a plausible voice. Timbre, accent, age impression, pitch range, and speaking style remain recognizably the same across short and long sentences and across early, middle, and late turns. Automated speaker-similarity and acoustic measures remain within the accepted reference/Spike 005 envelope, and no words or background content from the reference recording leak into unrelated output.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The clone sounds like another person, drifts toward a generic accent or different age/style, changes identity between chunks or turns, or reproduces reference wording/noise that was not requested.

**Stakes:** High

**Source:** The Qwen3-TTS authors evaluate cloning with both content WER and speaker-embedding cosine similarity; current voice-reconstruction research recommends pairing subjective perceived identity/intelligibility with objective measures because a single MOS or embedding score is not sufficient. RayMe Spike 005 adds the missing session-order stability gate. ([Qwen3-TTS evaluation](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS#evaluation), [Sanchez et al., Interspeech 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21343), [RayMe Spike 005](../../spikes/005-faster-qwen3-tts-longitudinal-quality/README.md))

#### Dimension: Spoken-message integrity and clean completion

**Good (domain expert would accept):** Listeners can recover the exact intended message, including names, numbers, negation, and the final word. RayMe STT remains near the accepted WER baseline without early-to-late regression; every segment reaches natural EOS at a plausible duration with no duplicated phrase, reference-text leakage, or clipped/swallowed ending.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** Words are substituted, omitted, repeated, slurred, or buried; the final syllable disappears; a short sentence loops or runs toward the token cap; or a prompt/reference mismatch produces speech unrelated to the target.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** ASA/ANSI S3.50 evaluates TTS intelligibility by what words listeners recover and normalized edit distance; Qwen3-TTS reports WER for content consistency and long speech; upstream issue #105 reports occasional swallowed endings; RayMe's mismatched-transcript probe produced an 81.92-second token-cap runaway. ([ASA/ANSI S3.50-2013 (R2022)](https://webstore.ansi.org/standards/asa/asaansis3502013r2022), [Qwen3-TTS technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15621), [faster-qwen3-tts issue #105](https://github.com/andimarafioti/faster-qwen3-tts/issues/105), [RayMe Spike 004a](../../spikes/004-a-faster-qwen3-tts-06b-cuda/README.md))

#### Dimension: Natural call delivery and invisible joins

**Good (domain expert would accept):** Speech has human-like phrasing, stress, pauses, loudness, pitch movement, and emotional restraint appropriate to an ordinary phone call. Chunk and text-segment boundaries are not audible as clicks, gaps, clipped phonemes, sudden pitch resets, or changes of mood; quality remains stable in early/middle/late listening samples.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** Delivery becomes robotic, overacted, flat, muffled, whisper-like, metallic, or noisy; cadence fights the sentence meaning; or adjacent chunks sound like different takes or different speakers.

**Stakes:** High

**Source:** ITU-T P.800 is the in-force foundation for subjective telephone transmission-quality testing; upstream issue #96 documents pitch, tone, and style discontinuity when text chunks are synthesized independently; RayMe's accepted listening reel explicitly gates progressive muffling, whisper, noise, metallic sound, and identity loss. ([ITU-T P.800](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-P.800), [faster-qwen3-tts issue #96](https://github.com/andimarafioti/faster-qwen3-tts/issues/96), [RayMe listening checkpoint](../../spikes/005-faster-qwen3-tts-longitudinal-quality/HUMAN-LISTENING-CHECKPOINT.md))

#### Dimension: Live turn-taking, audio supply, and interruption

**Good (domain expert would accept):** The first playable audio arrives before synthesis completes and close to the accepted OMEN baseline; generation continues faster than playout through a capacity-two queue without growing chunk debt, starvation gaps, or a whole-turn backlog. Barge-in and hangup stop audible speech and generation promptly; late chunks are discarded and cannot produce a normal completion or contaminate the next turn. Loading and voice-prewarm state is visible before the caller expects speech.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The caller waits for a whole WAV, hears widening gaps, or receives stale audio after interrupt; the producer outruns an unbounded queue; cancelled speech completes normally; or the UI claims the call is ready/listening while the voice is still loading or stuck.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** Qwen3-TTS is designed for streaming first-packet emission, but the product-level contract comes from RayMe's live-call invariants and accepted capacity-two completion/interruption probe: first consumption at 387 ms while production continued for 24.0 seconds, and cancellation stopped within 278 ms with no normal completion. ([Qwen3-TTS technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15621), [RayMe live-call invariants](../../LIVE-CALL-INVARIANTS.md), [RayMe Spike 006](../../spikes/006-faster-qwen3-tts-live-stream-contract/README.md))

#### Dimension: Reference consent, provenance, and private handling

**Good (domain expert would accept):** Every saved clone records who supplied/owns the reference, that person's permission for this use, the source and matching transcript, and the intended scope. Reference audio, transcript, prompt tensors, and generated samples stay access-controlled on the self-hosted system, have a deliberate retention/deletion path, are not repurposed for training or sharing, and are never used to mislead a listener about who is speaking.

**Bad (domain expert would flag):** The reference was scraped, copied, or supplied by someone without authority; consent or source cannot be shown; private voice material persists in temp files/logs/caches after deletion; or a clone is exposed outside the agreed LAN/test context without disclosure and review.

**Stakes:** Critical

**Source:** The FTC identifies voice appropriation, fraud, and misuse of biometric data/creative content as concrete voice-cloning harms, and separately emphasizes control over how voice recordings are obtained, retained, accessed, and reused. NIST's identity-proofing rules are not binding on RayMe, but their explicit-consent, use-disclosure, retention, and deletion controls are a sound conservative benchmark for voice data stewardship. ([FTC voice-cloning harms](https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2023/11/preventing-harms-ai-enabled-voice-cloning), [FTC voice-data privacy guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/06/hey-alexa-what-are-you-doing-my-data), [NIST SP 800-63A-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63a.html))

### Known Failure Modes in This Domain

- **Bad-reference runaway or leakage:** blank/grossly mismatched reference text breaks full-ICL conditioning, causing repeated or unrelated speech, reference-content leakage, implausible duration, or a token-cap run instead of natural EOS.
- **Longitudinal identity and signal collapse:** repeated turns progressively lose loudness, high-frequency energy, intelligibility, accent, or speaker likeness and end as muffled speech, whisper, near-silence, or noise even though isolated first-turn samples sound good.
- **Boundary and ending defects:** independent text segments reset pitch/emotion/style; codec joins click, gap, or clip phonemes; the tail of an otherwise valid sentence is swallowed. These low-frequency faults require many-turn and boundary-focused sampling rather than one showcase WAV.
- **Live-stream/control failure:** generation is accidentally collected before playback, blocks while each chunk plays, starves the call, fills an unbounded backlog, or ignores cancellation so late audio and a false normal completion leak after barge-in/hangup.

### Regulatory / Compliance Context

No sector-specific healthcare, financial, employment, or public-service regulation is identified for this LAN-only, self-hosted, non-commercial RayMe deployment. That limited context reduces exposure; it does not make another person's voice recording ethically ownerless or automatically settle which laws apply.

For EU-context use, the GDPR defines personal data broadly as information relating to an identifiable person and defines biometric data as data from specific technical processing that allows or confirms unique identification. Its special-category rule is specifically for biometric data processed to uniquely identify a person. It also contains a purely personal/household exemption, but whether a particular reference, recording, participant, publication, or later commercial use falls within it is fact-specific. ([GDPR Articles 2, 4, and 9](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj))

In the United States, the FTC has identified deceptive impersonation, voice appropriation, weak access control, repurposing, and indefinite retention as concrete concerns. State voice/biometric and call-recording rules vary. This spec therefore makes reference provenance, explicit permission, purpose limitation, LAN access control, and deletion product gates rather than asserting that one statute definitely governs. Any use involving a third party's voice, a real outside caller, publication, monetization, identity verification, or a child requires a fresh jurisdiction-specific legal/privacy review before expansion.

## 2. Framework Decision

**Selected Framework:** Official Faster Qwen3-TTS native Python API in a RayMe-supervised Windows subprocess

**Version:** `faster-qwen3-tts==0.3.2` / upstream tag `v0.3.2` at commit `a70afc0f81f7f5f8801c3227968f1102f43f211c`; model `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base` at an exact deployed Hugging Face snapshot revision

**Rationale:**
The native pull-based generator gives RayMe direct control of first-audio timing, capacity-two backpressure, prompt caching, cancellation, and generation ceilings. A RayMe-owned spawned worker preserves that control while containing CUDA faults and stuck generation, follows the established VoxCPM2 isolation pattern, and keeps one public API and one resident TTS model. The accepted RTX 3060 spikes validated this runtime and native streaming API directly.

**Alternatives Considered:**

| Framework | Ruled Out Because |
|-----------|------------------|
| Native API in the AI-backend process | Lower structural overhead, but CUDA faults and stuck generation can poison or block the call backend and are harder to terminate safely. |
| Upstream CLI or OpenAI-compatible HTTP server | Adds a second public/service lifecycle, duplicates deployment surfaces, and weakens RayMe's per-call streaming and cancellation control. |
| LangChain, LlamaIndex, generic agent/TTS wrapper | Adds no useful inference capability and risks hiding the pull-stream and backpressure semantics that are product invariants. |

**Vendor Lock-In Accepted:** Partial — voice prompt/model behavior is Qwen3-specific, while worker IPC, the RayMe streaming adapter, call events, bounded playout, and saved-voice surfaces remain engine-neutral.

### Core Imports
```python
from __future__ import annotations

from collections.abc import Generator
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Any

import numpy as np
import soundfile as sf
import torch
from faster_qwen3_tts import FasterQwen3TTS
```

### Entry Point Pattern
```python
MODEL_REVISION = "fd4b254389122332181a7c3db7f27e918eec64e3"
CHUNK_SIZE = 4  # 4 codec steps ~= 320-333 ms; RayMe buffers >=600 ms before playout


def load_runtime(model_dir: Path) -> FasterQwen3TTS:
    if not torch.cuda.is_available():
        raise RuntimeError("Qwen3-TTS 1.7B requires CUDA")
    model = FasterQwen3TTS.from_pretrained(
        str(model_dir),
        device="cuda",
        dtype=torch.bfloat16,
        attn_implementation="sdpa",
        max_seq_len=1536,
        backend="torch",
    )
    model.warmup(prefill_len=100)  # captures the CUDA graphs once
    return model


def prepare_icl_prompt(
    model: FasterQwen3TTS,
    reference_path: Path,
    reference_text: str,
):
    """Build one reusable full-ICL prompt inside the owning worker."""
    reference_text = reference_text.strip()
    if not reference_text:
        raise ValueError("Reference transcript is required")
    audio, sample_rate = sf.read(
        reference_path,
        dtype="float32",
        always_2d=False,
    )
    audio = np.asarray(audio, dtype=np.float32)
    if audio.ndim > 1:
        audio = audio.mean(axis=1)
    # Mirrors faster-qwen3-tts append_silence=True and prevents last-phoneme bleed.
    audio = np.concatenate(
        [audio, np.zeros(round(sample_rate * 0.5), dtype=np.float32)]
    )
    return model.model.create_voice_clone_prompt(
        ref_audio=(audio, int(sample_rate)),
        ref_text=reference_text,
        x_vector_only_mode=False,
    )


def stream_prepared_voice(
    model: FasterQwen3TTS,
    *,
    text: str,
    reference_text: str,
    prompt_items: list[Any],
    max_new_tokens: int,
) -> Generator[tuple[np.ndarray, int, dict], None, None]:
    stream = model.generate_voice_clone_streaming(
        text=text,
        language="English",
        ref_text=reference_text,
        voice_clone_prompt=prompt_items,
        chunk_size=CHUNK_SIZE,
        max_new_tokens=max_new_tokens,
        xvec_only=False,
        non_streaming_mode=True,  # full current segment prefill; audio still streams
        append_silence=True,
        parity_mode=False,
        temperature=0.9,
        top_k=50,
        top_p=1.0,
        do_sample=True,
        repetition_penalty=1.05,
    )
    try:
        yield from stream
    finally:
        stream.close()
```

This code belongs inside `app.models.tts_qwen3_worker`, never in the FastAPI
process. The production adapter converts each yielded NumPy chunk immediately
to the existing `TtsAudioChunk` contract and does not collect the generator,
call `generate_voice_clone()`, or write a whole-turn WAV first.

### Key Abstractions
| Concept | What It Is | When You Use It |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| `FasterQwen3TTS.from_pretrained()` | Loads Qwen3-TTS, constructs static KV caches, and prepares the CUDA-graph wrappers. | Once per spawned worker/model residency, using the immutable local snapshot directory. |
| `warmup(prefill_len=100)` | Captures predictor and talker CUDA graphs. It is idempotent for one model instance. | During visible model `loading`, before the adapter reports `resident`. |
| `create_voice_clone_prompt()` / `voice_clone_prompt` | Extracts reusable speaker embedding and full ICL reference codes from matching audio and transcript. | During visible per-voice `prewarming`; retain only the bounded selected-voice prompt in the worker. |
| `generate_voice_clone_streaming()` | Synchronous pull generator yielding `(audio_chunk, sample_rate, timing)` while generation continues. | Every preview and call segment; pull on the worker's generation thread and bridge through a capacity-two queue. |
| `chunk_size` and timing dict | Four codec steps yielded about 320 ms of audio on OMEN; timing includes chunk index, steps, prefill/decode time, total steps, and a best-effort `is_final`. | For TTFA, backpressure, output ceilings, and final metrics. Generator exhaustion—not `is_final`—is the authoritative normal end. |
| `non_streaming_mode=True` | Prefills all text for the current synthesis segment; it does **not** disable audio streaming. | Use for the accepted ICL quality path, but only after RayMe has bounded the current segment. |

### Common Pitfalls
1. **A reference transcript is model context, not optional metadata.** Full ICL
   (`xvec_only=False`) requires an accurate transcript. The known wrong
   transcript produced an 81.92-second token-cap runaway; blank or grossly
   mismatched pairs must be rejected before prompt extraction or generation.
2. **Pull-based is not automatically live.** Generation advances only when the
   caller requests the next chunk. Playing or awaiting each chunk inline stops
   generation/playout overlap; collecting the generator first destroys early
   playback. Use capacity-two producer/consumer overlap and true backpressure.
3. **Python task cancellation does not stop CUDA work in a thread.** Cancelling
   `asyncio.to_thread()` abandons the awaitable but not the worker thread. Give
   the spawned worker an explicit cancel command/event, check it between native
   yields, and terminate the process if it does not acknowledge within two
   seconds.
4. **The upstream prompt cache is path-keyed and unbounded.** Writing every
   request to a new temp filename causes misses and growth. Build a precomputed
   ICL prompt once, key RayMe's bounded cache by content/transcript/model hash,
   and clear it with model residency. Never serialize its GPU tensors through
   JSON.
5. **CUDA graphs are mutable single-stream state.** Do not run concurrent
   generations on one instance, load CUDA in the parent before Windows spawn,
   rely on CPU fallback, use `torch<=2.5.0`, or allow a mismatched driver/wheel.
   Serialize one active generation and fail the engine without taking down
   WebRTC/STT. Also treat generator exhaustion as completion: an exact multiple
   of `chunk_size` can end without a yielded timing record marked `is_final=True`.

## 4. Implementation Guidance

**Model Configuration:**

| Setting | Locked initial value | Reason |
|---------|----------------------|--------|
| Runtime | `faster-qwen3-tts==0.3.2`, commit `a70afc...` | Exact runtime that passed OMEN gates. |
| Model | immutable local snapshot of `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base`, revision `fd4b254...` | Prevent a mutable Hub update from changing quality or VRAM. |
| Backend/device | `backend="torch"`, `device="cuda"`, `dtype=torch.bfloat16`, `attn_implementation="sdpa"` | Accepted RTX 3060 CUDA-graph path; no FlashAttention dependency or CPU fallback. |
| Static cache | `max_seq_len=1536` | Preserves ample headroom above RayMe's bounded ICL prompt plus 384 generated codec steps while keeping the worker below the RTX 3060 allocator budget. |
| Clone mode | `xvec_only=False`, exact `ref_text`, precomputed ICL prompt, `append_silence=True` | Highest-fidelity selected voice-cloning path and clean reference ending. |
| Text/audio streaming | `language="English"`, `non_streaming_mode=True`, `chunk_size=4`, `parity_mode=False` | Approximately 320 ms native chunks preserve sustained RTF headroom across long calls; RayMe accumulates at least 600 ms before playout for smoothness. |
| Sampling | `temperature=0.9`, `top_k=50`, `top_p=1.0`, `do_sample=True`, `repetition_penalty=1.05`, `min_new_tokens=2` | Upstream defaults used by the accepted runtime. Use a different recorded random seed per segment, not one repeated production seed. |

Use a text-relative generation ceiling rather than upstream's permissive
`max_new_tokens=2048`. The initial policy should segment assistant speech at a
natural boundary no larger than 60 words, estimate `expected_seconds =
max(1.0, word_count / 2.2)`, set `hard_audio_seconds = min(32.0,
max(6.0, expected_seconds * 2.25 + 2.0))`, and round
`max_new_tokens = ceil(hard_audio_seconds * 12 / 4) * 4` with a global maximum
of 384. Stop and fail the segment if emitted audio exceeds the same duration
ceiling or generation reaches the token ceiling. Record `natural_eos=false`;
never continue partial runaway audio as a successful turn. These constants are
initial release gates and may change only with repeatable soak evidence.

**Core Pattern:**

Use the existing VoxCPM2 adapter/worker shape, but fix the cancellation boundary
rather than copying its sequential worker loop literally:

1. `Qwen3TtsAdapter.load()` spawns
   `sys.executable -m app.models.tts_qwen3_worker` with Windows spawn semantics,
   asks it to load the immutable local snapshot, and exposes `loading` until the
   worker reports CUDA, version, model revision, graph warmup, and sample rate.
2. The worker is the only process that imports/initializes the CUDA model. It
   permits one active generation. A small stdin reader thread accepts `cancel`
   while the CUDA-owning main/generation thread is inside the synchronous pull
   generator; stdout events remain serialized.
3. `prewarm(voice)` validates the matching transcript, builds `prompt_items`
   once, and atomically replaces the worker's selected-voice cache. Model
   `resident` and voice-prompt `prewarming/ready/failed` are separate states.
4. `stream(request)` sends only the target text, selected voice cache key,
   bounded token ceiling, and a request/turn id. The worker yields one validated
   chunk event at a time. The adapter converts it immediately to
   `TtsAudioChunk`; there is no `generate_voice_clone()` fallback.
5. `CallSession` bridges the blocking iterator to a capacity-two queue. A
   producer thread must block on a real bounded put (for example,
   `asyncio.run_coroutine_threadsafe(queue.put(item), loop).result()` with
   cancellation checks); `loop.call_soon_threadsafe(queue.put_nowait, item)` is
   not backpressure and will lose chunks when full.
6. On barge-in, hangup, engine switch, or session close, mark the turn cancelled,
   stop outbound playout, invoke `adapter.cancel(request_id)`, keep draining only
   protocol terminal state, and discard any late chunk. The worker checks its
   event before and after every upstream yield, closes the generator, and emits
   exactly one `cancelled` terminal event. If it cannot acknowledge within two
   seconds, terminate it; do not emit normal `ai_done` or replay the text.
7. On unload, terminate the worker, release the precomputed prompt and model,
   and let the model manager report no resident TTS until a new engine succeeds.

The upstream stream is faster than realtime only when generation and playout
overlap. Keep RayMe's bounded startup/jitter buffer, but emit first playback
before stream completion in the deliberately slow-stream regression. Preserve
the existing VoxCPM2 regression that prohibits whole-synthesis fallback.

**Tool Use:**

- `huggingface_hub.snapshot_download()` is deployment-time model acquisition;
  the service worker runs `local_files_only=True`/uses the resolved local path.
- `soundfile` decodes the saved reference and serializes each native float32
  chunk into the existing WAV-chunk transport. Reject empty, non-finite,
  near-silent, or invalid-rate chunks before playout.
- RayMe's existing Whisper STT supplies transcript alignment preflight. Compare
  normalized lowercase lexical tokens, ignoring punctuation/case. As an initial
  gross-mismatch gate, reject only when both token coverage is below `0.45` and
  normalized edit similarity is below `0.50`; preserve the STT transcript and
  score for evidence, not as a silent replacement for the user's transcript.
  Thresholds must pass punctuation/casing and accented-English fixtures while
  rejecting the known unrelated transcript.
- Pydantic validates worker IPC; `pynvml`/Torch metrics prove CUDA and VRAM.
  No vector database, agent framework, remote TTS API, or second service is
  required.

**State Management:**

Persist saved voice identity, reference audio, exact reference transcript, and
canonical `engine_id="qwen3_1_7b"` through RayMe's existing voice records. Do
not persist prompt tensors. Translate/migrate historical `qwen3_0_6b` data
explicitly; never let that id silently mean 1.7B.

The worker owns ephemeral state:

- model revision and CUDA-graph readiness;
- one active request id and cancellation event;
- a bounded selected-voice prompt cache (capacity one is sufficient for the
  one-hot call path);
- a cache key over SHA-256(reference bytes), normalized exact transcript,
  model revision, ICL mode, and appended-silence policy.

Any audio or transcript edit changes the key and returns prompt state to
`prewarming`. Engine switch/worker exit drops the cache. Health/status exposes
model `idle/loading/resident/unavailable` separately from prompt
`none/prewarming/ready/failed`, with timestamps and sanitized error codes. Logs
include request id, engine, hashed voice key, load/prewarm/TTFA/RTF, queue high
water, chunk counts, ceiling/cancel reason, GPU memory, and terminal event—but
never reference audio, full transcripts, or generated private speech.

**Context Window Strategy:**

Qwen's context here is the target synthesis segment plus reference text/audio
codes, not RayMe's whole conversation. Conversation history remains with the
assistant LLM. Feed TTS only a punctuation-safe bounded current segment and
reuse the selected voice prompt. Do not concatenate prior turns, earlier audio,
or multiple assistant chunks into the clone prompt. Validate target text and
prompt size against `max_seq_len=1536`; split before synthesis rather than
allowing the static cache to terminate generation. Keep adjacent text in
natural sentence/phrase segments to reduce the pitch/style discontinuity
reported for unrelated independent chunks, while never waiting for the full
assistant response.

## 4b. AI Systems Best Practices

> Adapted to a local streaming speech model. Generic LLM prompt/RAG/cost advice
> does not apply; the relevant controls are validated worker events, clone-prompt
> integrity, bounded acoustic state, and live-call latency.

### 4b.1 Validated Worker Protocol

The upstream tuple is trusted only inside the spawned worker. Convert it to a
versioned, request-scoped event and validate it before constructing
`TtsAudioChunk`. A compact Pydantic shape is appropriate at this process
boundary:

```python
from typing import Literal

from pydantic import BaseModel, Field


class QwenChunkEvent(BaseModel):
    schema_version: Literal[1] = 1
    event: Literal["chunk"]
    request_id: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=128)
    chunk_index: int = Field(ge=0)
    wav_b64: str = Field(min_length=1)
    sample_rate: Literal[24000]
    duration_ms: float = Field(gt=0, le=2000)
    generated_at_ms: float = Field(ge=0)
    total_steps_so_far: int = Field(ge=1, le=384)


class QwenTerminalEvent(BaseModel):
    schema_version: Literal[1] = 1
    event: Literal["done", "cancelled", "error"]
    request_id: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=128)
    chunk_count: int = Field(ge=0)
    natural_eos: bool = False
    error_code: str | None = None
```

Enforce monotonic chunk indexes and generated times, exact request id, decoded
byte limits, cumulative duration ceiling, and exactly one terminal event in the
adapter state machine. A malformed or wrong-request event is a worker protocol
failure: terminate the worker, log only the sanitized code/metadata, and mark
the engine unavailable. Do not retry a synthesis after any audio was played;
that would repeat speech. Before first audio, recovery may require an explicit
visible reload, never a hidden whole-synthesis fallback.

### 4b.2 Async-First Live Streaming

The library itself is synchronous. Keep CUDA and generator iteration in the
spawned worker, keep blocking pipe iteration off the FastAPI/aiortc event loop,
and keep the thread-to-async bridge bounded at two chunks. Await chunk enqueue
and outbound playout asynchronously. The common mistake is assuming task
cancellation stops `to_thread`; it does not. Cancellation is complete only
after the worker acknowledges `cancelled` (or is terminated), playout is
drained, and late events are rejected. Stream audio for live UX; await only
bounded readiness, terminal state, and final playout. Never call
`asyncio.run()` from RayMe's running event loop.

### 4b.3 Clone-Prompt Discipline

Keep four inputs separate: target text, explicit language, immutable reference
audio, and its matching reference transcript. The transcript describes the
reference recording; it is never conversation context, an instruction, or a
substitute for target text. Phase 09 does not use Base-model `instruct`.
Normalize only for comparison/cache keys—pass the user-approved exact transcript
to ICL. Require `language="English"`, bound target segment words and output
tokens explicitly, and keep `append_silence=True`. Never silently change to
x-vector-only mode when transcript validation fails; that changes cloning
quality and violates the selected product path.

### 4b.4 Bounded Acoustic Context and Cache Lifetime

The reusable prompt is the only cross-turn TTS context. Prior generated codec
tokens, waveform chunks, sampling history, and target text must not carry into
the next turn. The upstream static caches are reset by each generation prefill;
the 50-turn deterministic anchors are the regression that proves this remains
true. Limit the prompt cache to the selected voice, invalidate it on content,
transcript, model, or policy changes, and drop it on model unload. Limit each
producer queue to two chunks and each synthesis to the text-relative token/audio
ceiling. Never cache completed conversational audio as a latency shortcut.

### 4b.5 Cost and Latency Budget

There is no per-character or per-call vendor charge: inference, prompt
extraction, and transcript preflight are self-hosted on OMEN. The material
costs are the immutable 4.54 GB model snapshot, RTX 3060 VRAM/energy, and load
time. Exact snapshot caching and one selected-voice prompt eliminate repeated
download/extraction; semantic caching and cheaper-model routing are inappropriate
because the user explicitly selected 1.7B cloning quality.

Initial release budgets come from the accepted evidence:

| Budget | Target / hard gate |
|--------|--------------------|
| Cold model load | Visible throughout; accepted cold evidence was 92.7 s plus 2.59 s graph warmup. Do not allow call speech until resident. |
| Selected-voice prewarm | Visible and complete before first call response; target <=15 s on cached model. |
| Native hot first chunk | Target <=500 ms and hard median <=600 ms at `chunk_size=4`; caller playout retains its independent <=1.25 s hard gate and bounded >=600 ms startup buffer. |
| RayMe first playback | Before stream completion, normally <=1.25 s bounded startup wait; immediate timing emitted separately. |
| Sustained generation | RTFx >=1.05 every accepted sample; target median >=1.25 (accepted 1.46). No positive growing chunk debt. |
| Queue | Capacity/high-water <=2; never proportional to turn length. |
| Interrupt | Worker stop acknowledgement <2 s, target <=500 ms; accepted real case 278 ms with <=1 in-flight chunk. |
| Memory | Torch reserved <=5.75 GiB and whole-system RTX 3060 usage below the configured VRAM budget; accepted reserved 5,604 MiB and system use 8,128–8,348 MiB. |
| Longitudinal drift | Preserve 50/50 natural EOS, zero reserved-memory growth, early-to-late WER delta <=0.15, RMS delta <=3 dB, centroid ratio >=0.70, and TTFA growth <=200 ms. |

Record native first-chunk time, first enqueue/`ai_audio_started`, generation
completion, and playout completion as different timestamps. A fast first chunk
does not prove smooth or complete playback, and final metrics must never be
copied back into the immediate event.

## 6. Guardrails

Online guards are limited to failures that can corrupt a call, strand state,
leak voice data, or poison the process. Transcript alignment runs at save or
prewarm and is cached by the immutable voice/model key; it is not repeated on
every spoken segment.

### Online (Real-Time)

| Guardrail | Trigger | Intervention |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Runtime/model attestation | Package/source commit, model revision, Torch/CUDA/device, CUDA-only parameter evidence, or one-hot residency differs from the pinned contract. | **Block Qwen only:** do not report resident/ready, unload or terminate the worker, expose a sanitized `qwen_runtime_identity_failed`, and leave STT/WebRTC/other engines available. Never fall back to CPU or 0.6B. |
| Reference integrity preflight | Transcript is blank; audio is invalid/near-silent; or both normalized token coverage `<0.45` and edit similarity `<0.50`. | **Block save/prewarm/call synthesis:** preserve the user's data, show an actionable mismatch message, and require transcript/audio correction. Never silently switch to x-vector-only cloning. |
| Generation and output ceiling | Target exceeds safe segment/context bounds, token total reaches the text-relative ceiling, cumulative audio exceeds `hard_audio_seconds`, natural EOS is false, or repetition/reference leakage sentinel fires. | **Stop and fail the segment:** signal cancellation, drain/discard queued partial audio, emit sanitized `qwen_generation_ceiling`, and do not emit normal `ai_done` or persist a complete speech artifact. |
| Worker protocol/audio validation | Wrong request id, non-monotonic index/time, duplicate terminal event, malformed/base64-oversized event, invalid sample rate, NaN/Inf, empty/near-silent chunk, or cumulative bytes/duration violation. | **Terminate the worker and block the engine:** discard the event and later chunks, emit one sanitized engine-scoped failure, and require visible reload. Do not retry after any audio played. |
| Bounded live bridge | Producer queue reaches capacity two, consumer slows, or generation supply drops below realtime. | **Backpressure, never enlarge:** block producer on the bounded put and keep the live invariant. Record high-water/RTF/underflow. If sustained RTFx falls below `1.05`, fail/flag Qwen live viability explicitly after the bounded current segment; never wait for whole synthesis or route silently. |
| Cancellation watchdog | Barge-in, interrupt, hangup, engine switch, or session close occurs; matching worker has not acknowledged within 2 s. | **Cancel and recover:** stop/drain playout immediately, reject all later request events, suppress `ai_done`/complete persistence, and terminate the worker at the hard timeout. Return session to `listening` or `ended`. |
| Voice-data boundary | Reference path escapes RayMe storage, voice is not the selected saved record, cache key mismatches content/transcript/model, or a log/evidence field contains raw audio/full transcript/private path/token. | **Reject/redact:** block the request or evidence publication, invalidate prompt cache, emit only opaque ids/hashes and stable codes, and clear temp/prompt state on unload/delete. |

### Offline (Flywheel)

| Metric | Sampling Strategy | Action on Degradation |
|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| Fifty-turn longitudinal stability | Run the full `hot-50-turn` gate on every Qwen runtime/model/CUDA/driver/worker/caching change and before every promoted deployment; retain early/middle/late scalar evidence and opt-in reel. | Block release on any Critical threshold; classify the first failing turn/metric, compare with Spike 005, and require a fresh 50-turn pass after the fix. |
| Intelligibility, endings, and runaway incidence | Run RayMe STT/WER on every reference-set output; always include final-word, number/name/negation, long text, and known mismatch/ceiling cases. | Block release for invalid audio, failed normal EOS, late WER `>0.20`, early-to-late WER delta `>0.15`, overall WER `>0.10`, or any known mismatch that reaches generation. Add every new swallowed/repeated/leaked case to the dataset. |
| Clone likeness and join quality | Product owner reviews blinded short/long and early/middle/late samples for each release; automated local speaker-similarity/join trends run on the same samples. | Block release if human median is `<4`, any sample `<3`, or any progressive identity/join defect is heard. Investigate scorer/human disagreement; never average it away. |
| Latency, realtime supply, and queue debt | Recompute medians and per-sample RTF/TTFA/high-water/underflow from raw OMEN events on every release; in normal use, retain scalar metrics for all Qwen turns. | Flag a single threshold miss; mark Qwen not live-ready/block promotion on any fallback/unbounded/underflow event or repeated (3 of 5) first-playback/RTF misses. Do not solve with whole-response buffering. |
| Memory/residency drift | Record worker Torch allocated/reserved and whole-system NVML use at load, after prewarm, early, middle, late, cancellation, switch, and unload. | Block release above 5.75 GiB Torch reserved, 11 GiB system budget, 256 MiB early-to-late growth, or more than one resident TTS engine; inspect worker/cache lifecycle. |
| Failure containment and error hygiene | Preserve all non-success stable error codes and metadata; leak-scan every decision-ready JSON/log excerpt. Weight crash, timeout, malformed protocol, cancellation timeout, transcript reject, and ceiling breach at 100%. | Any traceback/private path/audio/transcript/token leak blocks evidence and release. Three Qwen failures in ten attempted turns mark the engine unhealthy until visible reload; backend/other-engine loss is an incident. |
| Field-call anomalies | Sample all anomaly-signaled turns and the first, 10th, 25th, and 50th Qwen turns of any long session; use only already-enabled saved AI audio for listening. | Add reproducible failures to the 20-scenario manifest, preserve sanitized metadata, run the focused regression plus full 50-turn gate when the pattern is longitudinal. Never auto-save mic/reference audio for monitoring. |

## 7. Production Monitoring

**Tracing Tool:** RayMe-local structured JSON logs and request-scoped call events,
correlated by `session_id`, `turn_id`, `request_id`, `engine_id`, and hashed
`voice_key`, with `/health`, `/webrtc/status`, model/prompt state, Torch/NVML
gauges, and schema-versioned Phase 09 evidence JSON. No Phoenix/hosted tracer and
no raw audio or full transcript in traces.

**Key Metrics to Track:**

1. **Identity/readiness:** runtime commit/version, model revision, Torch/CUDA/GPU,
   worker pid/generation, model state, prompt state, load/prewarm duration, cache
   hit/miss/eviction, and resident TTS count.
2. **Immediate latency:** native first-chunk ms, first-enqueue ms,
   `ai_audio_started` ms, startup-buffer chunks/audio/wait, and whether playback
   preceded producer completion.
3. **Final supply/playout:** chunk count, generated/playable audio ms, generation
   and playout completion ms, RTFx, queue high-water/block time, active-turn
   underflow/silence-frame count, inter-chunk availability debt, join flags,
   natural EOS, token/audio ceiling margin, and whole-synthesis/fallback flags.
4. **Cancellation/state:** reason, signal-to-playout-stop and worker-ack ms,
   in-flight and discarded late chunks, forced termination, terminal event,
   persisted speech row count, recovered session state, and next-call success.
5. **Quality/drift:** sampled WER and final-word pass, RMS/active RMS, silence,
   clipping, spectral centroid/high-frequency ratio/flatness, speaker-similarity
   trend, Torch/NVML memory, error code, and turn ordinal.

Immediate and final carriers remain physically separate. Monitoring may join
them by request id for analysis, but it must never rewrite the immediate event
with values learned at completion.

**Alert Thresholds:**

| Severity | Condition | Response |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Release blocker / engine unavailable | Identity/CUDA/model mismatch; CPU fallback; resident TTS count `>1`; whole-synthesis call; queue high-water `>2`; first playback not before completion; protocol violation; late audible/enqueued chunk; normal `ai_done` after cancel; private-data leak. | Stop Qwen readiness immediately, preserve sanitized evidence, keep the rest of RayMe alive, and require a verified redeploy/fix. |
| Hard runtime alert | Cancellation acknowledgement `>=2 s`; Torch reserved `>5.75 GiB`; whole-system GPU use `>11 GiB`; output reaches ceiling/non-natural EOS; active-turn playout underflow; worker crash/hang. | Stop/terminate the worker, mark prompt not ready/engine unhealthy, suppress successful completion, and surface a stable Qwen error. |
| Performance warning | Hot native median TTFA `>500 ms`; RayMe first playback `>1.25 s`; any sustained RTFx `<1.05`; median RTFx `<1.25`; TTFA growth `>200 ms`; or three performance misses in five Qwen turns. | Flag locally and attach the request ids to the next offline evidence run; mark not live-ready if RTF/underflow repeats. Never increase to whole-turn buffering. |
| Longitudinal quality blocker | Late WER `>0.20`, late-minus-early `>0.15`, overall WER `>0.10`, absolute RMS drift `>3 dB`, centroid ratio `<0.70`, flatness growth `>0.15`, RTF ratio `<0.75`, reserved-memory growth `>256 MiB`, speaker-similarity median drop `>0.05`, or any human-detected progressive collapse. | Remove Qwen from ready status/promotion, retain early/middle/late evidence, and require root-cause repair plus a new 50-turn and listening pass. |
| Product-readiness warning | Cached-model voice prewarm `>15 s`, state remains `loading`/`prewarming` beyond its timeout, Qwen error rate reaches three of ten attempted turns, or another call cannot start after failure. | Show the honest state, mark the engine unavailable rather than freezing the call, and run containment/recovery diagnostics. |

**Smart Sampling Strategy:**

- Record privacy-safe scalar/event metrics for 100% of Qwen turns; raw reference
  audio, mic audio, full transcript, and generated speech are never copied into
  monitoring. Listening uses only AI audio the user already chose to save.
- Retain 100% of anomaly metadata for transcript rejection, ceiling/non-EOS,
  first playback over 1.25 s, RTFx below 1.05, any underflow, worker failure,
  cancellation at/over 500 ms, forced termination, late chunk, state mismatch,
  VRAM breach, or sanitized-error event.
- Stratify longitudinal review by turn order: first 1–5, middle 23–27, last
  46–50, plus deterministic anchors 1/10/20/30/40/50. This directly targets the
  reported “starts right, then degrades” failure instead of over-sampling good
  first turns.
- On every decision-ready release, review the fixed short/long clone samples,
  consequential-word/endings set, one normal deployed call, one barge-in call,
  and the 1/25/46/50 listening reel. Repeat after any runtime/model/CUDA driver,
  prompt-preparation, chunking, sampling, cache, queue, or playout change.
- If an objective metric passes but the user reports muffling, whispering,
  noise, identity drift, gaps, or swallowed endings, the report wins: preserve
  that saved-output sample with consent, add a minimized fixture/label, and
  expand the flywheel before changing thresholds.
